## Full description
Tactica finds the moments in your own games where you missed a win, then turns them into puzzles. Import your games from Chess.com or Lichess, let the built-in Stockfish engine study them on your device, and train on the exact positions you got wrong.

- Personal puzzles from your own mistakes: forced mates, winning material and only-moves
- "Why is that wrong?" shows how the opponent punishes a wrong move
- See the continuation after every puzzle, and what really happened in your game
- Coach tab with your average centipawn loss, blunders per game, weakest phase and the tactics you miss most
- Hint, Show and Solve when you are stuck
- Only new games are analysed. Everything is saved on your device, with backup and restore
- Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking
- Resizable panel: shrink it for a bigger board, expand it to read explanations

Tactica is open source (GPL-3.0). Source code: https://github.com/javiersubatin/tactica. Not affiliated with Chess.com or Lichess.
