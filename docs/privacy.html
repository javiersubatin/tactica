Tactica Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Tactica does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

What stays on your device. Your settings, the games you import, the analysis results and your puzzle progress are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app or using "Clear" in the import window removes them. "Export backup" creates a file that you control.

Network requests. When you tap Import, the app sends the username you typed to Chess.com (api.chess.com) or Lichess (lichess.org) to download your public games. Those services handle that request under their own privacy policies. Game analysis is done by the Stockfish engine inside the app, on your device. No games are uploaded anywhere else.

Children. The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
